What AR Boxing Is

AR Boxing is a boxing-inspired fitness app for iPhone. It uses camera-guided, on-device movement tracking to show punch targets, defense prompts, timers, custom combos, workout history, and user-created gameplay recordings.

AR Boxing is for fitness and entertainment. It is not professional boxing instruction, medical advice, physical therapy, diagnosis, or a substitute for a qualified trainer, coach, or healthcare professional.

Safety

You are responsible for using AR Boxing safely.
