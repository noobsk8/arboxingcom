FAQ

Boxing combo questions.

Does this builder generate random combinations?

This website tool lets you choose the movements and their order manually. Build up to 12 supported moves, including punches, defense, and rest options, then preview the sequence.

Can I share a combination by link or QR code?

Yes. The builder creates a public link and a downloadable QR code for your sequence. Share either so another person can preview the combo and use the Open in AR Boxing button.

What happens if the app is not installed?

The shared link opens a combo preview with an App Store download option. After installing AR Boxing, return to the shared link or scan the QR code again to open the combo.

Can I save and practice the combo in AR Boxing?

AR Boxing supports saving custom combinations and practicing supported sequences in the app. The website builder is free; interactive training in the iPhone app requires a subscription.

Can I include slips and rolls?

Yes. Choose supported slips, rolls, and guard movements alongside your punches. Start with a short sequence and follow the orthodox defense examples when adding a defensive finish.

What is a simple combination to start with?

Try a lead jab followed by a rear cross, then reset your guard. Our beginner combo guide provides more practice ideas. These prompts do not replace qualified instruction.
