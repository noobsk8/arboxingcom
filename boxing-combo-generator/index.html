FAQ

Boxing combo questions.

What is a boxing combo generator?

It creates a short sequence of punches and defensive movements you can use as a training idea for shadow boxing or bag work.

Can I save generated combos in AR Boxing?

AR Boxing includes a custom combo builder where you can create and save supported punch and defense sequences in the app.

Are these combinations coaching advice?

No. Generated combinations are training prompts. They do not replace qualified boxing instruction or professional coaching.

Can I include defense in my combinations?

Yes. AR Boxing supports defensive movements such as slips, rolls, and guard blocks in custom training sequences.
