Shadow Boxing Combos for Beginners

Shadow boxing lets you practice punches, defense, rhythm, and movement without a bag or partner. The goal is controlled movement, not throwing as hard as possible.

Watch the reference: Nate Bower Fitness’s beginner shadow boxing workout.

1-2: Find your rhythm

Throw the lead jab and rear cross, then reset your feet and guard.

AR Boxing sequence: ljh-rch

1-1-2: Build volume

Use two jabs to establish distance before the cross. Keep both jabs straight and bring the hand home each time.

AR Boxing sequence: ljh-ljh-rch

1-2-3-slip-2: Punch, defend, return

Finish the 1-2-3, make a small slip, and return with the rear cross.

AR Boxing sequence: ljh-rch-lhh-sl-rch

A beginner three-round format

Round one is technique, round two is rhythm, and round three is movement. Add a small step or angle after the final punch only if you can stay balanced.
