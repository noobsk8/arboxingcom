Body-Head Boxing Combos: Change Levels With Control

Changing levels asks you to move between body and head targets while keeping your stance, guard, and balance. Lower your level with your knees instead of folding at the waist.

Fundamentals reference: Boxing Canada’s beginner manual.

Body jab to head cross

Start with the lead jab to the body, return to guard, and bring the rear cross upstairs.

AR Boxing sequence: ljb-rch

Body jab-cross-hook

Use the lead body jab to change levels, bring the rear cross to the head, and finish with the lead hook.

AR Boxing sequence: ljb-rch-lhh

Jab-cross-body hook-lead hook

Start high with the jab and cross, change levels for the lead body hook, then return upstairs with the lead hook.

AR Boxing sequence: ljh-rch-lhb-lhh

How to practice

Start without power and pause briefly after the level change. Keep your eyes forward, protect your head with the opposite hand, and return to your stance after the body shot.
