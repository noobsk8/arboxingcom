FAQ

Boxing timer questions.

Can I control a boxing timer with gloves on?

Yes. AR Boxing supports punch gestures for timer controls when you are within camera range. A left hook pauses or resumes the timer, and an uppercut restarts a round, as shown in the controls above.

Do I need to stay in camera range during heavy-bag rounds?

You need to be in camera view for gesture controls. You can work on the bag while the round runs, then return to camera range to control the timer.

Can I use the timer without a rhythm workout?

Yes. AR Boxing includes a dedicated timer mode for timed rounds and rest periods independently from rhythm training. It can structure heavy-bag rounds or shadowboxing practice.

Do I need an Apple Watch or voice commands?

No. The camera-based gesture controls do not require an Apple Watch, wearable sensors, or spoken commands. Place your iPhone so it can see your movements when you use a control.

Is the timer free to use in the app?

The AR Boxing app requires a subscription to access training features, including the timer. There is no free trial; review the current subscription terms in the app before purchasing.
