5 Beginner Boxing Combos You Can Practice at Home

Short combinations are easier to remember, repeat, and improve. Start slowly, return to guard after every punch, and add speed only when your balance stays controlled.

Watch the reference: FightCamp’s beginner boxing combinations video.

1. The 1-2

The 1-2 is a lead jab followed by a rear cross. Use the jab to establish rhythm, then rotate through the rear hand without falling forward.

AR Boxing sequence: ljh-rch

2. The 2-3-2

Throw the rear cross, lead hook, and rear cross. Keep the hook compact and let the final cross come from a balanced stance.

AR Boxing sequence: rch-lhh-rch

3. The 1-2-3

The jab-cross-lead hook combines straight punches with a hook. Finish with your chin protected and your feet underneath you.

AR Boxing sequence: ljh-rch-lhh

4. The 1-2-3-2

Add a rear cross after the lead hook. Do not rush the last punch; reset your guard before repeating.

AR Boxing sequence: ljh-rch-lhh-rch

5. The 1-2-slip-2

After the jab and cross, make a small slip to the left before returning with the rear cross. Keep the movement compact instead of bending at the waist.

AR Boxing sequence: ljh-rch-sl-rch

How to practice

Work each combination for 30 seconds, rest for 30 seconds, and repeat for three rounds. Use the AR Boxing combo builder to create, preview, and share a sequence.
