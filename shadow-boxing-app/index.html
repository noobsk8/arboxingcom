FAQ

Shadow boxing app questions.

Can I use AR Boxing for home workouts without a bag?

Yes. AR Boxing supports interactive shadowboxing using your iPhone camera. You do not need a heavy bag, but you do need enough clear space for punches and defensive movements.

How does the workout become interactive?

Visual targets and movement prompts give you a sequence to react to. AR Boxing responds to supported camera-based interactions while you practice punches, combinations, slips, rolls, and guard movements.

Where should a beginner start?

Choose Basic Training and begin at a pace you can control. For a simple practice structure, follow our three-round home shadowboxing plan. Learn technique with qualified instruction and prioritize balance and returning to guard.

Do I need sensors or an Apple Watch?

No. AR Boxing uses the iPhone front camera for supported movement tracking. Place it securely with a clear view of your movements and keep the surrounding space free of obstacles.

Can I choose my own combinations?

Yes. Use the boxing combo builder to choose a sequence, then open supported combos in AR Boxing. You can also use the hands-free timer for independent rounds.

Does it judge my boxing form?

No. Target responses are not a technique assessment. AR Boxing helps structure practice, but does not certify form or replace a boxing coach.
