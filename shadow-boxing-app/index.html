FAQ

Shadow boxing app questions.

Is AR Boxing a shadow boxing app?

Yes. AR Boxing is designed for interactive shadow boxing workouts on iPhone, with supported punch and defensive movement recognition.

Do I need a punching bag?

No. You can train without a bag. A heavy bag is optional for timer and bag-style rounds.

Do I need sensors or an Apple Watch?

No additional wearable sensor is required for camera-based training. Set up your iPhone where the camera can see your movement.

Does it judge my boxing form?

No. AR Boxing detects interaction with workout targets. It does not certify proper boxing technique or replace coaching.
