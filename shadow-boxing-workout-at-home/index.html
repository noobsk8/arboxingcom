Shadow Boxing Workout at Home: A Simple 3-Round Plan

Clear enough space to move safely, warm up, and keep the first round controlled. Shadow boxing is practice, not a test of how hard you can punch.

Workout inspiration: Nate Bower Fitness’s beginner workout and Healthline’s shadowboxing guide.

Round 1: Fundamentals

Repeat the 1-2 at a relaxed pace. Focus on your stance, straight punches, and returning both hands to guard.

Combo: ljh-rch

Round 2: Combinations

Alternate between the 1-2-3 and 2-3-2. Take a breath and reset after every sequence.

Combos: ljh-rch-lhh and rch-lhh-rch

Round 3: Defense and rhythm

Use the 1-2-slip-2 combination. Add a small step or angle after the final cross only if you can stay balanced.

Combo: ljh-rch-sl-rch
