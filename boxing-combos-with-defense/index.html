Boxing Combos With Defense

A combination does not have to end with the last punch. Adding a slip, roll, or guard movement gives you a reset and helps you practice what happens after you attack.

Technique reference: England Boxing Level 2 Coaching Handbook.

1-2-slip-2

Throw the jab-cross, slip left, and counter with the rear cross. Keep the slip small and stay over your feet.

AR Boxing sequence: ljh-rch-sl-rch

1-2-roll-3

Throw the jab-cross, roll under an imagined return hook, and come back with the lead hook. Bend your knees rather than folding at the waist.

AR Boxing sequence: ljh-rch-rolll-lhh

1-2-3-slip-2

Build from the classic 1-2-3, then move your head and finish with the rear cross.

AR Boxing sequence: ljh-rch-lhh-sl-rch

How to train

Practice punches only, then add defense at half speed, and finally perform the complete sequence at a steady pace. Reset your guard before starting again.
