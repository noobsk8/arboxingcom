## What is the 1-2-3?

The 1-2-3 is the lead jab, rear cross, and lead hook. It is a common beginner sequence because it combines straight punches with a hook and teaches you to stay connected through the combination.

**AR Boxing sequence:** `ljh-rch-lhh`

## Step-by-step

Start in your boxing stance with both hands near your face. Send the lead jab straight out and bring it back. Throw the rear cross while rotating the rear hip and shoulder. Finish with a compact lead hook, turning through the hips without swinging your arm wide.

After the hook, return both hands to guard and re-establish your stance. The reset is part of the drill.

## Three ways to practice it

First, shadow box the combination slowly for technique. Next, repeat it with a steady rhythm and a full exhale on each punch. Finally, add a small defensive exit such as a slip or roll after the hook.

## Common beginner mistakes

- Reaching with the jab instead of returning it to guard.
- Letting the rear heel and hip stay locked during the cross.
- Swinging the hook from too far away.
- Standing still after the final punch.

Start with clean movement and add speed only when you can keep your balance.
