## Why change levels?

Body-head combinations ask you to change the target while keeping your stance, guard, and balance. The movement comes from bending the knees and lowering your level, not from folding at the waist.

## Combo 1: Body jab to head cross

Start with the lead jab to the body, return to guard, and bring the rear cross upstairs.

**AR Boxing sequence:** `ljb-rch`

## Combo 2: Body jab-cross-hook

Use the lead body jab to change levels, bring the rear cross to the head, and finish with the lead hook.

**AR Boxing sequence:** `ljb-rch-lhh`

## Combo 3: Jab-cross-body hook-lead hook

Start high with the jab and cross, change levels for the lead body hook, then return upstairs with the lead hook.

**AR Boxing sequence:** `ljh-rch-lhb-lhh`

## How to practice

Start without power and pause briefly after the level change. Keep your eyes forward, protect your head with the opposite hand, and return to your stance after the body shot. Train each combination for 20 to 30 seconds before resting.

The [AR Boxing combo builder](/boxing-combo-generator/) lets you place head, body, low, defense, rest, and repeat moves in an ordered sequence. You can then use the generated QR code to share the workout.

These are general training prompts. A qualified coach can help you adapt the movements to your stance, experience, and equipment.
