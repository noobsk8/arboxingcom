## Introduction

Boxing combinations do not need to be complicated to be useful. Beginners usually get more from a short sequence they can repeat with balance, a steady guard, and controlled breathing than from a long combination they cannot remember.

These five combinations are simple training prompts for shadow boxing or bag work. Start slowly, return to your guard after every punch, and add speed only when your movement stays controlled.

## Combo 1: 1-2

The 1-2 is a lead jab followed by a rear cross. Use the jab to establish rhythm, then rotate through the rear hand without falling forward.

**AR Boxing sequence:** `ljh-rch`

## Combo 2: 2-3-2

Throw the rear cross, lead hook, and rear cross. Keep the hook compact and let the final cross come from a balanced stance.

**AR Boxing sequence:** `rch-lhh-rch`

## Combo 3: 1-2-3

The jab-cross-lead hook is one of the clearest beginner combinations. Finish with your chin protected and your feet underneath you.

**AR Boxing sequence:** `ljh-rch-lhh`

## Combo 4: 1-2-3-2

Add a rear cross after the lead hook. Do not rush the last punch; reset your guard before repeating.

**AR Boxing sequence:** `ljh-rch-lhh-rch`

## Combo 5: 1-2-slip-2

After the jab and cross, make a small slip to the left before returning with the rear cross. Keep the movement compact instead of bending at the waist.

**AR Boxing sequence:** `ljh-rch-sl-rch`

## How to practice

Work each combination for 30 seconds, rest for 30 seconds, and repeat for three rounds. Practice the first round slowly, the second at a steady pace, and the third only as quickly as you can stay in control.
