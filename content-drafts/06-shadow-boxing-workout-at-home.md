## Before you start

Clear enough space to move safely, warm up, and keep the first round controlled. Shadow boxing is practice, not a test of how hard you can punch. Keep your hands up, breathe steadily, and stop if you feel pain, dizziness, or unusual shortness of breath.

## Round 1: Fundamentals

Repeat the 1-2 at a relaxed pace. Focus on your stance, straight punches, and returning both hands to guard.

**Combo:** `ljh-rch`

## Round 2: Combinations

Alternate between the 1-2-3 and 2-3-2. Take a breath and reset after every sequence.

**Combos:** `ljh-rch-lhh` and `rch-lhh-rch`

## Round 3: Defense and rhythm

Use the 1-2-slip-2 combination. Add a small step or angle after the final cross if you can stay balanced.

**Combo:** `ljh-rch-sl-rch`

Rest for 30 to 60 seconds between rounds. Beginners can complete one cycle; more experienced users can repeat the plan after a longer break.
