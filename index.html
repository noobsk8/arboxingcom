FAQ

Quick answers before your first round.

Do I need boxing equipment?

No special tracking equipment is required. AR Boxing uses your iPhone camera for movement tracking, and a heavy bag is optional.

Is this a shadow boxing app?

Yes. You can use AR Boxing for interactive shadow boxing workouts without a bag. The app uses your iPhone camera to recognize supported punches and defensive movements as you train.

Do I need boxing sensors or an Apple Watch?

No additional wearable sensor is required for camera-based training. Set up your iPhone where its camera can see your movements and begin your workout.

Can I use it as a boxing round timer?

Yes. AR Boxing includes a boxing timer designed for training rounds. Supported punch gestures let you control the timer without taking off your gloves to tap the screen.

Can I create custom boxing combinations?

Yes. Use the combo editor to build training sequences with supported punches, defensive movements, rests, repetitions, and tempos.

Is AR Boxing available for Android?

AR Boxing is currently built for iPhone. Android availability is not announced for launch.

Does AR Boxing analyze my boxing technique?

AR Boxing detects interaction with workout targets. It does not certify or guarantee proper boxing technique.

Is my camera video uploaded?

Workout video is not uploaded for movement analysis. Movement tracking is processed on-device.
