FAQ

Quick answers before your first round.

Do I need boxing equipment?

No special tracking equipment is required. AR Boxing uses the iPhone camera for movement tracking, and a heavy bag is optional.

Do I need an account?

No account is required.

Does AR Boxing analyze my boxing technique?

AR Boxing detects interaction with workout targets. It does not certify or guarantee proper boxing technique.

Is my camera video uploaded?

Workout video is not uploaded for movement analysis. Movement tracking is processed on-device.

Can I use it with a heavy bag?

Yes. AR Boxing includes bag target placement and a dedicated Boxing Timer designed around heavy-bag-style training.

Can I create my own combinations?

Yes. Users can build and save custom boxing combinations and workout classes.
