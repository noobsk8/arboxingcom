FAQ

Punch tracking questions.

Is AR Boxing a boxing punch tracker app?

AR Boxing uses your iPhone camera to recognize supported punches and defensive movements during interactive workouts.

Does it count every punch perfectly?

No. AR Boxing focuses on interaction with supported workout targets. It should not be treated as certified punch-counting equipment.

Do I need an Apple Watch or wearable sensor?

No additional wearable sensor is required for camera-based training. Place your iPhone where the camera can see your movement.

Does it analyze my boxing form?

No. AR Boxing does not certify technique or replace a coach. It provides interactive workout targets and broad training feedback.
