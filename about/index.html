About the app

AR Boxing is being built to make solo boxing training more interactive.

AR Boxing turns your iPhone into a camera-guided workout screen for boxing-inspired training, custom combinations, rhythm practice, and hands-free timer control.

The goal is simple: make shadow boxing, bag rounds, and combo practice feel less like staring at a timer and more like playing through a structured training session.

Why AR Boxing Exists

Most solo boxing workouts fall into one of two buckets: either you follow a basic interval timer, or you watch a video and try to keep up. AR Boxing is designed to sit between those experiences. It gives you visible targets, supported punch and defense cues, custom combinations, and timer controls that make sense when your gloves are already on.

The app is built for people training alone at home, in a garage, at the gym, or near a heavy bag who want more structure without buying extra hardware.

How It Works

  • Use your iPhone camera for supported movement recognition during workouts.
  • Practice jabs, crosses, hooks, uppercuts, body shots, slips, rolls, and guard blocks.
  • Build custom combinations with punches, defense, rests, repetitions, and tempo.
  • Use hands-free timer controls for round training when tapping the screen is awkward.

Privacy And Camera Use

AR Boxing is designed around on-device movement tracking. Workout video is not uploaded for movement analysis, and the website does not use advertising pixels, heatmaps, or session replay. The website uses privacy-friendly analytics to understand broad page visits, waitlist actions, and whether key pages are working.

You can read the full details in the Privacy Policy.

What AR Boxing Is Not

AR Boxing is a fitness and training tool. It does not replace qualified boxing instruction, professional coaching, medical advice, or safety supervision. App metrics should not be treated as certified punch scoring, technique certification, or proof of boxing skill.
